## 1. Organic post (personal profile)

LinkedIn shows the first ~210 characters before "…see more", so the first two lines have to work alone.

> My AI agent was 40 minutes into a refactor. Then my Mac fell asleep.
>
> So I built keepAwake, a tiny macOS menu bar app that keeps your Mac awake exactly as long as you need it. ☕
>
> 🤖 Connect Claude Code or Cursor in one click. The Mac stays awake while the agent works and sleeps again when it's done. Any MCP client can ask for more time too.
> ⏱ Or pick a time yourself: 15 min, 1 h, 4 h, up to 30 days, or Always, with a live countdown in the menu bar
> 🔋 Battery guard turns it off below 10%, so you never come back to a dead laptop
> 💻 Optional: keep running with the lid closed, or keep the display on for demos
>
> No Dock icon, no background daemons. There's also a CLI and a keepawake:// URL scheme for scripts and Shortcuts.
>
> Free and open source. Download link in the first comment 👇
>
> What's the worst moment your Mac ever decided to take a nap?
>
> #macOS #AIagents #ClaudeCode #Cursor #DeveloperTools #OpenSource
